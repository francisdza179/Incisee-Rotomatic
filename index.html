Who We Are

India's Precision
Web Converting Specialists

Incisee Rotomatic Pvt. Ltd. is a Chennai-based, IATF 16949:2016 certified manufacturer within the Incisee Group. We specialise in custom rotary and flatbed die-cutting, web converting, and precision component fabrication — serving the most demanding industries in India and globally. Our core production is dedicated to the Automotive industry, where our components go into battery packs, powertrains, and thermal management systems.

⚙️
Rotary & Flatbed Die-Cutting

High-speed, high-precision cutting of films, foams, membranes, and specialty materials to exact tolerances.

🔬
Advanced Material Processing

Expert handling of ePTFE, VHB, silicone, thermal interface materials, and proprietary adhesive systems.

🏆
OEM-Grade Quality Assurance

IATF 16949:2016 quality management system ensuring every part meets automotive-level reliability standards.
