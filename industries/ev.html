Precision Components for EV Manufacturing

Modern electric vehicles demand components that are precise down to fractions of a millimetre – protecting sensitive battery packs, managing thermal runaway efficiently, and ensuring environmental sealing across the vehicle's lifetime.

Incisee Rotomatic's precision die-cutting capabilities enable us to deliver EV components with tight dimensional tolerances, clean-cut edges, and consistent adhesive application – all critical for high-volume battery pack and powertrain assembly.

Thermal Interface Pads

Die-cut thermal interface material (TIM) pads for battery cell-to-cooling plate heat transfer, power inverter dissipation, and onboard charger thermal management.

Electronic Die-Cuts

Custom die-cut components for HV battery busbar insulation, BMS circuit isolation, inverter module gasketing, and high-voltage connector sealing in EV assemblies.

ePTFE Venting Membranes

Adhesive-backed ePTFE vent membranes for EV battery enclosure pressure equalisation, motor housing venting, and charging connector breathers – waterproof and breathable.

Protective Films

Precision die-cut surface protection films for battery cell tab insulation, busbar shielding, and enclosure surfaces during EV pack assembly and shipping.

Silicone Compression Pads

Electrically insulating silicone pads for high-voltage component isolation, battery module compression padding, and vibration dampening in EV powertrain assemblies.

Applications
  • – Battery pack thermal management
  • – EV powertrain sealing
  • – High-voltage component isolation
  • – Charging station enclosures
  • – Battery Management Systems (BMS)
