Precision Components for Telecom Manufacturing

Modern electronics demand components that are precise down to fractions of a millimetre – protecting sensitive circuits, managing heat efficiently, and ensuring environmental sealing across the product lifetime.

Incisee Rotomatic's precision die-cutting capabilities enable us to deliver electronics components with tight dimensional tolerances, clean-cut edges, and consistent adhesive application – all critical for high-volume electronics assembly.

Thermal Interface Pads

Die-cut thermal interface material (TIM) pads for CPU/GPU heat management, power modules, and LED driver boards. Available in various conductivity grades.
Electronic Die-Cuts

Custom die-cut components for PCB isolation, component gasketing, EMI shielding layers, and sensor sealing in electronic assemblies.
ePTFE Venting Membranes

Adhesive-backed ePTFE vent membranes for electronics enclosures, portable devices, and outdoor control panels – waterproof and breathable.
Protective Films

Precision die-cut surface protection films for PCBs, display panels, and enclosure surfaces during electronics manufacturing and shipping.
Silicone Compression Pads

Electrically insulating silicone pads for power device isolation, keyboard backing, and vibration dampening in electronics assemblies.
Applications
  • – Consumer electronics (smartphones, wearables)
  • – Industrial control panels
  • – LED lighting systems
  • – Power electronics modules
  • – Automotive ECUs
