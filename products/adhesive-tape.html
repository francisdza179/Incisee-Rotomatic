Adhesive Tape

Single-sided adhesive tapes in acrylic, rubber, and silicone grades for masking, bundling, surface protection, and high-temperature applications.

Industry-standard single-sided tapes precision converted to your specifications. Includes polyimide (Kapton) tapes for solder masking, polyester tapes for coil winding, and specialized high-temperature masking tapes for automotive painting processes.

Technical Specifications

Typical specification range — custom grades available to specific application requirements. Contact our team for material certification data.

Adhesive TypeAcrylic / Rubber / Silicone
Backing MaterialPET / Polyimide / Paper / Cloth / PTFE
Total Thickness0.03 mm – 0.50 mm
Adhesion to Steel3 N/cm – 12 N/cm (typical)
Temperature Range-30°C to +150°C / up to +260°C (silicone/polyimide)
Dielectric StrengthUp to 10 kV (polyimide)
Delivery FormDie-cut shapes / slit rolls / sheets
CertificationsIATF 16949:2016 (process); RoHS compliant

Key Properties

✦
High-Temp Performance

Polyimide and silicone tapes withstand solder wave, powder coating, and painting processes up to 260°C.

✦
Clean Removal

Engineered adhesives that remove cleanly without residue after high-temperature or long-duration applications.

✦
Die-Cut Precision

Custom geometries with tight tolerances for gasketing, shielding, and protective masking applications.

Where It's Used

Applications of Adhesive Tape

→ PCB Solder Masking (Polyimide)
→ Automotive Paint Masking
→ EV Battery Cell Insulation
→ Coil Winding / Transformer Insulation
→ Bundling & Holding Applications
