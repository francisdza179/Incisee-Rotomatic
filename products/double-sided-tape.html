Double-Sided Tape

Precision die-cut double-sided tapes in acrylic, rubber, and silicone adhesive systems for permanent mounting, splicing, and assembly applications.

Our double-sided tape portfolio covers light-duty mounting to high-strength structural bonding. Available in transfer tape, foam carrier, and film carrier constructions — die-cut to any shape with tight tolerance registration.

🚗 Automotive 💡 Electronics 🏭 Industrial 🏥 Medical

Typical specification range — custom grades available to specific application requirements. Contact our team for material certification data.

CarrierNone (transfer) / PET film / Acrylic foam / PE foam

Key Properties

📋
Precision Die-Cutting

Complex geometries and tight tolerances (±0.10 mm) for automated assembly lines.

🔥
Temperature Versatility

Acrylic for general purpose, silicone for high-temp, rubber for high-tack applications.

🧩
Multi-Substrate Bonding

Excellent adhesion to plastics, metals, glass, and powder-coated surfaces without primers.
