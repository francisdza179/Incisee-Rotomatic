EP Valves

Explosion-proof pressure relief valves designed to protect sealed automotive and electronic enclosures from dangerous pressure build-up. Certified for hazardous environments.

Advanced safety venting solutions engineered to prevent catastrophic enclosure failure by releasing excess pressure in a controlled manner. Each EP valve model is calibrated to specific crack pressures and available in multiple mounting configurations including threaded, flanged, and snap-fit.
