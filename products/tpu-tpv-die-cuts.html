TPU/TPV Die-Cuts

Precision die-cut thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) and thermoplastic vulcanizate (TPV) parts for electrical insulation, sealing, gasketing, and vibration dampening.

TPU and TPV combine the performance characteristics of rubber with the processing advantages of thermoplastics. Our die-cutting delivers consistent, burr-free parts from sheet stock in a range of hardness grades for automotive, electronics, and industrial applications.

Technical Specifications

Typical specification range — custom grades available to specific application requirements. Contact our team for material certification data.

MaterialsTPU (Polyurethane) / TPV (PP/EPDM blend)
Hardness55 – 95 Shore A
Thickness Range0.1 mm – 6.0 mm
Temperature Range-40°C to +120°C (TPV) / -50°C to +80°C (TPU)
Tensile Strength> 20 MPa (TPU) / > 8 MPa (TPV)
Abrasion ResistanceExcellent (TPU)
Chemical ResistanceGood (TPV — oils, greases)
Dielectric Strength> 15 kV/mm
Delivery FormDie-cut parts / sheets / strips
