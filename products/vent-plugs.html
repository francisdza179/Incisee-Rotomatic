Pressure Relief

Vent Plugs

Precision pressure relief solutions for enclosures that demand controlled venting. Our vent plugs combine engineered membranes with robust housing to equalize internal pressure, block liquid ingress, and maintain IP rating.

Available in explosion proof and ordinary pressure relief valve configurations for automotive, EV, telecom, electronics, and industrial applications.

Product Range

Pressure Relief Valve Variants

Explosion Proof Pressure Relief Valves

Rugged metal-housing valves with explosion-proof membrane technology. Designed for hazardous environments where flammable gases or dust may be present. Prevents flame propagation while maintaining reliable pressure equalization. Available in multiple thread sizes and aluminium alloy configurations.

EP Valve 400
EP Valve 600
EP Valve AL 200
EP Valve AL M20
EP Valve M42
Inquire about Explosion Proof Valves →

Ordinary Pressure Relief Valves

Cost-effective PRV and snap-in vent solutions for general-purpose enclosure protection. Ideal for automotive electronics, LED lighting, telecom cabinets, and consumer appliance applications. Available in threaded PRV models (M8, M12, M16, M20) and push-fit snap-in vent designs for fast tool-free installation.

PRV M8×1.5
PRV M12×1.5
PRV M16×1.5
PRV M20×1.5
Snap-in Vent 7d
Snap-in Vent 10d
