VHB Tapes

High-strength acrylic foam tapes for structural bonding of panels, trim, and components. Vibration and shock resistant with excellent weatherproofing.

Very High Bond (VHB) tapes replace mechanical fasteners, rivets, and liquid adhesives in permanent bonding applications. Our precision die-cutting delivers ready-to-apply VHB parts with exact geometry and release liner options for automated or manual assembly.

Key Properties

💪
Structural Strength

Replaces rivets, screws, and welds in many applications with bond strength exceeding substrate material.

🌀
Vibration Dampening

Acrylic foam core absorbs vibration and shock, reducing noise and preventing fatigue failure.

🌦️
Weatherproof

Excellent resistance to moisture, UV, temperature cycling, and environmental exposure over long service life.
